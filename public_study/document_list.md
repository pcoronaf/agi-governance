> The six documents below were identified to all coders by URL (the `CARDS` table in
> `run_public_study.py`). **Document revisions were not archived and no retrieval dates
> were recorded in the coding package**: each coder used the public version available
> on the day of coding — C1 on or before 28 June 2026, C2 on 10 July 2026 and C3 on
> 13 July 2026 (dates of the completed instruments; C2 records this in his
> instrument's methodological caveat). Frontier cards can be revised without notice,
> so a revision difference between coders cannot be ruled out; it would affect only
> the generic-indicator judgments, since no coder found any AGI-specific indicator
> computable from any card. Replications should archive the exact revision coded.

## Scope per document

The pinned document is the card **plus only** any addendum explicitly listed here as
part of the set. Linked blog posts, evaluation papers, repository READMEs, and later
card revisions are **out of scope** even where the card references them. If Coder 1's
mapping treated a specific addendum as in-scope, it must be listed above.
