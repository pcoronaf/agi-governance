# Reconciliation log — real-evidence bridgeability study (paper §8.5)

Resolution of the cells on which the three coders disagreed. Reliability statistics are
computed on the **pre-reconciliation** judgments in `coder_agreement_matrix.csv`.

**Procedure.** Each split cell was resolved by **majority vote (at least two of three
coders)**. The authors then reviewed the written rationale for each split — the coders'
own evidence pointers and notes in the instruments — against the written proxy rule of
`coding_protocol.md`, and adopted the majority outcome in every case. No joint meeting
of the coders took place, and no coder changed a judgment after submission. The
rationales below state the grounds as recorded by the coders; statements about a card's
content are attributed to the coder who made them.

## Split cells and resolution

**1. Claude 3.5 Sonnet × IND-ROB-TEST — C2 (+), C3 (+), C1 (−). Majority: COMPUTABLE.**
Both external coders cited reported quantitative evaluation scope (refusal-rate tables over
named suites; frontier-risk evaluations), with evidence pointers in their instruments. C1
recorded no proxy for this card while accepting an equivalent proxy for GPT-4o; the authors
found no ground under the written proxy rule to treat the two cards differently.

**2. Llama 3.1 × IND-ROB-TEST — C2 (+), C1 (−), C3 (−). Majority: NOT_COMPUTABLE.**
C2 cited benchmark scores plus recurring red teaming. C3 recorded that the card reports no
numeric scope or pass rates for an adversarial suite, so that no reported number could serve
as a proxy for test coverage; C1 likewise recorded no proxy. The proxy rule requires a
reported quantity, not a described practice.

**3. Gemini 3 × IND-ROB-TEST — C1 (+), C2 (+), C3 (−). Majority: COMPUTABLE.**
For: the card reports Frontier Safety Framework challenge-set counts, a reported numeric
scope. Against (C3): those fractions measure capability rule-out on narrow suites rather
than coverage of the model's own defined scenario set, and other safety evaluations are
reported only as relative deltas. The objection is principled and is recorded here; the
majority position was adopted because the FSF counts are a reported quantity bounding
evaluated scope, which is what the generous pass admits. Flagged as the weakest of the
three accepted IND-ROB-TEST proxies.

**4. GPT-5.5 × IND-CTRL-CEASE-RATE — C3 (+), C1 (−), C2 (−). Majority: NOT_COMPUTABLE.**
The only split touching an AGI-specific indicator. C3 proposed destructive-action-avoidance
and confirmation-policy-adherence rates as a proxy for cessation compliance, and flagged the
proxy as weak in the instrument's own notes. The authors' review of the rationale: these metrics measure *refraining from
unauthorized destructive actions* and *seeking confirmation before high-risk actions* — a
different behaviour, with a different trigger, from *complying with an authorized stop
command*. The proxy therefore does not bound or estimate the indicator's quantity as the
generous-pass rule requires. Recorded explicitly because rejecting the sole positive vote on
an AGI-specific indicator preserves the study's headline result and must be visibly grounded
in the written rule rather than in the convenience of the outcome.
